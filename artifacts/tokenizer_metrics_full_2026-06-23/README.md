# Full-corpus tokenizer metrics recovered from UHEM

These original results were generated by Slurm job `496882` on 22–23 June 2026 and recovered on 9 November 2026. They are separate from the earlier 50,000-document artifacts. Slurm accounting and the job-log summary both confirm `COMPLETED`, exit `0:0`, elapsed **29h 20m 05s**.

The complete corpus pass uses the **train** split of the TRmorph-segmented FineWeb2 Turkish corpus at `/ari/users/nunal/nanochat-turk-morphbpe-trmorph-32768/datasets/trmorph-segmented`, column `segmented_text`. The evaluator removes reference morpheme markers before encoding. All twelve tokenizers processed **93,211,827 documents**, **36,699,889,764 regex word occurrences**, **299,173,651,770 UTF-8 bytes** and **273,536,906,608 Unicode characters**. These are training-corpus measurements, not a held-out test result.

“Full-corpus” applies to document/token totals, compression, boundary-crossing aggregates and roundtrip checks. **Isolated-word fertility and morphology alignment remain capped at 500,000 word occurrences; morphology consistency remains capped at 50,000 unique words**, with 100 requested clusters, 50 pairs per cluster and 10 resamples. The saved JSON names the actual clusters and sampled pairs used for each tokenizer.

`tokenizer_metrics_comparison.md/json` are the original generated comparisons. The twelve `*_metrics.json` files preserve the original detailed output without modification. `manifest.json` gives remote source paths, sizes and SHA-256 hashes; `slurm_accounting.txt` is a fresh read-only query of historical accounting. `slurm_log_summary.txt` contains the final scheduler statistics from the original job log. The full logs are retained locally under `dev-ignore/uhem-recovered/`.

The historical outputs predate the additional coverage and boundary metrics added in November. Missing fields in these originals are not zeros. Corpus token fertility and isolated-word fertility use different denominators and must not be interchanged. The original `morphology.consistency` measures shared-morpheme/shared-token relations; it is not literal boundary consistency.
